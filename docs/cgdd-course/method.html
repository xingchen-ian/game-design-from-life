不要选边。从一段有难度的过程做游戏。Do not pick a side. Start from one difficult process.

判断如何改变、感受如何发生，是同一段生活的两面。抽出玩家会反复做的最小循环，再用它自己的变量把世界长出来。How judgment changes and how feeling moves are two faces of the same lived process. Extract the smallest loop the player will repeat, then grow the world from that loop’s own variables.

你不再选择「情感经验路径」或「领域学习路径」。夏校的领域问题清单仍然可用——那是取样方式，不是第二条方法。 You no longer choose an emotion path or a domain-learning path. The summer-camp domain questions still work — they are a sampling tactic, not a second method.

一条链One chain

先冷凝，再展开Condense first, then expand

1

有难度的生活过程A difficult lived process

排队、争执、赶地铁、学帆船、钩针。难在制度、时间、身体、技艺或他人。Queuing, a fight, a missed train, learning to sail, crochet. Difficulty lives in rules, time, the body, skill, or other people.

2

同一张表读两面Read both faces on one sheet

判断：看错 → 看对 → 失败后下次看什么。感受：触发、张力、转折、高峰、余韵。Judgment: misread → reread → what you look at after failing. Feeling: trigger, tension, turn, peak, aftertaste.

3

最小互动循环The smallest loop

3–5 个动词 + 一次判断 + 一种反馈 + 再试一次。抽掉装饰，循环仍能被玩、被失败。3–5 verbs + one judgment + one feedback + a reason to try again. Strip the decoration; the loop must still play and fail.

4

变奏长成世界Variations grow a world

同一循环的合法难法，最好能站成一个地方。海峡，不是「第 3 关」。Legal ways to make the same loop harder should become places. A strait, not “level 3.”

禁令Do not

这四件事会让设计塌掉These four collapse the design

先发明世界Invent the world first

先锁循环，再让循环要求一个地方，再让这个地方要求一段历史。Lock the loop first. Let the loop demand a place. Let the place demand a history.

只写情绪，或只写训练Mood only, or drill only

抽掉判断，会变成气氛；抽掉感受，会变成训练软件。两面都要扛住。Drop judgment and you get atmosphere. Drop feeling and you get training software. The loop must carry both.

换皮或问答Skin or quiz

通用玩法套题材，或把知识点做成问答，都不是这个方法。A generic mechanic wearing your topic, or a quiz of facts, is not this method.

试玩只问一句Playtest asks one thing

他们开始用你的方式看，并以那种方式感受了吗？Did they start to see it your way, and feel it that way?

三轮试玩协议已经按这句话写好：沉默试玩看自我解释，出声思考数领域词还是数值词，反向教学对情感曲线。不要只验其中一面。The three-round playtest is already written for this: silent play for self-explanation, think-aloud for domain words vs. score words, teach-back for the emotion curve. Do not test only one face.
