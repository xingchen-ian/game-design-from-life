Mini-Project 1 · 无代码Mini-Project 1 · No Coding

用引导式问答，把知识变成可玩框架Turn Knowledge into a Playable Framework, by Guided Questioning

Game One 刻意保持低技术：不写代码，专注设计思维。你选一个真正理解的领域，通过一系列结构化问题，把它系统化为一个可玩的原型。Game One is deliberately low-tech: no code, pure design thinking. Pick a domain you truly understand, and a series of structured questions turns it into a playable prototype.

这是「来自生活的游戏设计」四步法的第 ①→② 步：从感受出发，抽象成动作 / 过程 / 空间 / 数据。 This is steps ①→② of the four-step method: from experience, abstracted into action / process / space / data.

Game One 路线图The Game One Roadmap

C1Framework
课程介绍与破冰；四步法总览；玩一个游戏并分析它的「体验来源」。Course intro & ice-breaking; the four-step method; play a game and analyze its experience source.
C2Framework
引导式创作工作坊：用问题把领域系统化为机制；AI 作为设计伙伴辅助头脑风暴与批判。Guided-questioning workshop: questions turn a domain into mechanics; AI as design partner for brainstorming & critique.
C3Framework
用户测试与情感等价验证：玩家是否体验到了你想要的感受？测试报告 + 迭代。User test & emotional-equivalence validation: did players feel what you intended? Test report + iteration.
