Part 1 · 定位Part 1 · Why This Way

为什么不是"让 AI 做个小游戏"Why Not Just "Let AI Make a Mini-Game"

01AI 能很快做出游戏，但常常是换皮AI can build a game fast — but it is often just reskinned

问题The problem

你给 AI 一个主题，它能生成一个能跑的游戏。但它常常是"通用玩法 + 你的题材"——把赛车、打怪、消消乐套上一层帆船或咖啡的皮。这类游戏好玩，但不表达你对这个领域的真实理解。Give AI a theme and it produces a working game. But it is usually "generic mechanic + your topic" — racing, fighting, or match-3 wearing a sailing or coffee skin. Those games are playable, but they do not express what you actually understand about the domain.

02夏校里的真实反馈：视角不是我想的那个Real feedback from the summer school: the camera was not what I imagined

观察The observation

很多学生说：AI 生成的游戏视角其实不是他设想的那样。根因是——他在设计阶段从没被要求说清楚"玩家应该从哪个角度、看见什么"。等 AI 猜错了，游戏已经做完了。Many students said the AI-generated camera was not what they had imagined. The root cause: they were never asked, during design, to state clearly "from what angle, and what should the player see." By the time AI guessed wrong, the game was already built.

所以这门课加了一整步：Step 7 视觉与镜头。视角不是美术问题，是核心判断能不能被看见的问题。That is why this course adds a whole step: Step 7 Visual & Camera. The camera is not an art choice — it decides whether your core judgment is visible at all.

03核心主张：游戏机制 = 你对领域的真实判断Core claim: game mechanics = your real judgment about a domain

主张The claim

帆船不是"开船"，是读风、调帆、判断航线；咖啡不是"冲咖啡"，是看粉的粗细、水的温度、流速。当你把这些判断变成数据、把环境对数据的干扰变成挑战，游戏才真正来自你的领域。Sailing is not "driving a boat"; it is reading the wind, trimming the sail, judging the course. Coffee is not "making coffee"; it is reading grind size, water temperature, flow rate. When you turn those judgments into data — and turn what disturbs that data into challenges — the game finally comes from your domain.

Part 2 · 三步流程Part 2 · The Three Sessions

整门课走三步The Whole Module in Three Sessions

1

INSIDE 分析INSIDE Analysis

选一个你真正了解的领域，拆解它的体验来源：感受、机制、叙事、结构。产出：你的领域选择。Pick a domain you truly know and break down where its experience comes from: feeling, mechanics, narrative, structure. Output: your domain choice.

2

Workbook 九步Workbook · Nine Steps

用结构化问题把领域判断拆成可运行的系统：数据、操作、挑战、视角、人机边界。产出：Brief（16 节）+ 系统图。Structured questions turn your domain judgment into a runnable system: data, actions, challenges, camera, human–AI boundary. Output: a 16-section brief + system graph.

3

AI 开发与记录AI Build & Log

把 Brief + 系统图 + 开发日志协议交给 AI，生成可玩 demo，并全程自动记录你的决策与反思。产出：游戏 + 项目网站 + 开发日志。Hand the brief + graph + log protocol to AI. It builds a playable demo and logs your decisions and reflections along the way. Output: game + project site + development log.

07学生做 vs AI 做：边界在哪You vs AI: where the line is

这门课不教编程，但也不让你把思考外包出去This course does not teach coding — but it will not let you outsource the thinking

✋ 你亲手做✋ You do by hand

  • 建立项目文件夹结构（理解项目由什么组成）Create the project folder structure (understand what a project is made of)
  • 填写 Workbook，写出 BriefFill the workbook and write your brief
  • 画系统图Draw the system graph
  • 把文件 push 到 GitHubPush your files to GitHub
  • 回答 AI 的反思提问，写下你的决策Answer AI's reflection questions and record your decisions

  • 写代码、调试、生成网站Write code, debug, generate the site
  • 整理规则与变量建议Organize rules and suggest variables

判断标准：涉及"知识框架和流程"的，你亲手做；纯技术实现苦力，交给 AI。The rule: anything about the knowledge framework or the process, you do by hand. Pure technical labor goes to AI.

Part 3 · 九步设计（Session 2 核心）Part 3 · Nine Steps (Session 2 Core)

把领域判断拆成可运行的系统Turning Domain Judgment into a Runnable System

08Step 1–4：从经验到反馈Steps 1–4: From Experience to Feedback

  • Step 1 领域采样Step 1 Domain Sampling — 你为什么了解它？新手通常误以为什么最重要？你第一次卡住是什么时候？Why do you know it? What do beginners wrongly assume matters most? When did you first get stuck?
  • Step 2 新手 / 高手差异Step 2 Novice vs Expert — 面对同一情境，新手和高手的判断差在哪？用一句话概括核心学习转变。In the same situation, how do novice and expert judgments differ? Summarize the core learning shift in one sentence.
  • Step 3 技能循环Step 3 Skill Loop — 观察 → 判断 → 行动 → 反馈 → 调整 → 熟练。玩家的每一次循环是什么？Observe → Judge → Act → Feedback → Adjust → Master. What is one full loop for your player?
  • Step 4 领域反馈表Step 4 Domain Feedback — 哪些反馈立刻出现？哪些要延迟？哪些不能用分数表示，必须用画面 / 声音 / 手感？Which feedback is immediate? Which is delayed? Which cannot be a number and must be shown through visuals, sound, or feel?

09Step 5：数据化抽象 —— 整个设计的枢纽Step 5: Data Abstraction — the Hub of the Whole Design

把数据分成三类，是后面所有设计的入口Sorting your data into three groups is the entry point for everything that follows

环境数据Environment Data

系统提供，玩家不能直接控制：风向、风力、洋流、礁石、时间。Supplied by the system, not directly controlled: wind direction, wind strength, current, rocks, time.

玩家可操作数据Player-Controlled Data

玩家的 action / input 能直接改变：船头方向、帆角、路线。Directly changed by the player's action/input: boat heading, sail angle, route.

系统计算结果System-Calculated Results

系统根据前两类算出：船速、漂移、稳定性、与目标距离。Computed from the first two: boat speed, drift, stability, distance to target.

关键一问：你的每个操作（按键 / 点击 / 拖动）分别改变哪个数据？写不出来，说明机制还没想清楚。The key question: which data does each of your controls change? If you cannot write it down, the mechanic is not yet clear.

10Step 6：挑战空间 —— 挑战来自数据，不是关卡Step 6: Challenge Space — Challenges Come from Data, Not Levels

从 Step 5 的"玩家可操作数据"倒推：环境中有哪些因素能影响它？那些因素就是你的挑战要素。Work backwards from Step 5's player-controlled data: what in the environment can affect it? Those factors are your challenge elements.

  • 船速 ← 风力、洋流 → 挑战：横流让航线持续偏移Boat speed ← wind, current → challenge: a cross current keeps pushing your course off
  • 帆角 ← 风向、风力 → 挑战：逆风目标不能直线到达Sail angle ← wind direction & strength → challenge: an upwind target cannot be reached directly
  • 航线 ← 洋流、礁石 → 挑战：安全路径变窄Route ← current, rocks → challenge: the safe path narrows
同一个因素的不同状态 = 难度阶梯。顺风（简单）→ 侧风 → 逆风（困难），玩家学会的是"读风与规划航线"，不是"手速更快"。Different states of the same factor = a difficulty ladder. Downwind (easy) → crosswind → upwind (hard). What the player learns is reading wind and planning a course — not faster reflexes.

11Step 7：视觉与镜头 —— 视角必须让核心判断被看见Step 7: Visual & Camera — the Camera Must Make the Core Judgment Visible

  • 视角Camera — 俯视 / 横版侧视 / 第一人称 / 第三人称跟随 / 等距？Top-down / side-scroll / first-person / third-person follow / isometric?
  • 为什么Why — 读风需要同时看见风向、船头、目标，所以俯视才成立；要"被带着走"的感觉，就得第三人称跟随。Reading wind requires seeing wind, bow, and target at once, so top-down works; for a "being carried along" feeling, you need third-person follow.
  • 维度与风格Dimension & style — 2D / 2.5D / 3D？像素 / 手绘 / 极简几何 / 写实？2D / 2.5D / 3D? Pixel / hand-drawn / minimal geometry / realistic?
  • 色彩与声音Color & sound — 明亮或昏暗？冷或暖？需要什么音效强化反馈？Bright or dark? Cool or warm? What sounds reinforce the feedback?

12Step 8：人机协作边界 —— 必须在 AI 动手前声明Step 8: Human–AI Boundary — Declare It Before AI Starts

  • 你保留的You keep — 核心机制、学习转变、胜负条件、核心变量。AI 不得擅自改。Core mechanic, learning shift, win/lose conditions, core variables. AI must not change them without asking.
  • 交给 AI 的You delegate — 技术实现、代码结构、视觉微调、音效实现。Technical implementation, code structure, visual polish, sound implementation.
  • 纠偏预案Correction plan — 如果 AI 把它做成了"普通小游戏"（只是题材换皮），你怎么发现？怎么拉回来？If AI turns it into a generic mini-game (theme only), how will you notice, and how will you pull it back?
为什么必须事前：等到开发完再反思，AI 已经越完界了。声明要写进 Brief 的第 7 节。Why it must come first: by the time you reflect after the build, AI has already crossed the line. Put the declaration in section 7 of your brief.

13系统图 + Brief：交给 AI 的两份文件The Graph + Brief: Two Files to Hand Over

  • 系统图System graph — 在共用 Canva 白板上画：挑战如何设置数据、玩家如何操作、系统如何计算、反馈如何帮助学习。Draw on the shared Canva board: how challenges configure data, how the player acts, how the system calculates, and how feedback supports learning.
  • Brief（16 节）Brief (16 sections) — Workbook 自动生成：项目信息、设计摘要、玩家循环、数据模型、挑战空间、视觉镜头、人机边界、规则、反馈优先级、首版范围、技术依赖、网站要求、开发日志协议、提交打包、AI 指令、验收清单。Generated automatically by the workbook: project identity, design summary, player loop, data model, challenge space, visual & camera, AI boundary, rules, feedback priorities, first-version scope, tech dependencies, site requirements, log protocol, packaging, AI instructions, acceptance checklist.

Part 4 · 技术要点Part 4 · Technical Essentials

四个最容易做错的地方Four Things That Go Wrong Most Often

14

数据三分类是枢纽The Three-Category Split Is the Hub

环境 → 玩家操作 → 系统计算。分错了，后面挑战和反馈全会跑偏。先分类，再设计其他。Environment → player action → system calculation. Get this wrong and challenges and feedback both drift. Classify first, design after.

15

挑战来自数据，不是关卡Challenges Come from Data, Not Levels

"第 3 关加更多敌人"不是挑战设计。真正的挑战是：哪个领域因素变了，会迫使玩家换一种判断方式。"Level 3 adds more enemies" is not challenge design. A real challenge: which domain factor, when changed, forces the player to judge differently.

16

视角让核心判断被看见The Camera Makes the Core Judgment Visible

选错视角，游戏直接废掉——玩家根本看不到该看的东西。这是夏校里最高频的翻车点。Pick the wrong camera and the game is broken — the player simply cannot see what matters. This was the most common failure in the summer school.

17

人机边界要事前声明Declare the Boundary Beforehand

事后反思 AI 已经改完了。边界写进 Brief，AI 才会把它当作约束而不是建议。After-the-fact reflection is too late — AI has already changed things. Put the boundary in the brief so AI treats it as a constraint, not a suggestion.

Session 3：与 AI 一起做完，并亲手交付Session 3: Finish It With AI — and Submit By Hand

18开发流程：三份输入 → 三类产出The Build Flow: Three Inputs → Three Outputs

输入Inputs

  • Brief（16 节）Brief (16 sections)
  • 系统图（Canva 链接或 PNG）System graph (Canva link or PNG)
  • 人机开发日志协议（Log Prompt，可复制）Human–AI Development Log Protocol (copyable prompt)

产出Outputs

  • 可玩网页游戏（HTML / CSS / JS / Canvas，无构建步骤）Playable web game (HTML / CSS / JS / Canvas, no build step)
  • 项目网站（相对路径，可直接静态托管）Project website (relative paths, static-hostable)
  • 开发日志 agent-development-log.md（AI 自动维护）agent-development-log.md (maintained automatically by AI)

19日志结构：AI 记流水，你写决策Log Structure: AI Logs the Trail, You Write the Decisions

  • Raw Interaction LogRaw Interaction Log — AI 自动记录：每次有意义的开发交互都追加一条，含时间、做了什么、为什么。Logged automatically by AI: every meaningful development interaction gets an entry with time, what was done, and why.
  • Reflection LogReflection Log — 里程碑时 AI 主动问你：你接受了 AI 哪些建议？拒绝或改了哪些？为什么？最终决策是谁做的？At milestones AI asks you: which AI suggestions did you accept? Which did you reject or change, and why? Who made the final decision?
这是你"独立决策"的证据——也是老师检查时最看重的部分。要如实写，包括失败和未完成。This is the evidence of your independent decisions — and the part your instructor reads most closely. Write honestly, including failures and unfinished work.

→ 打开开发日志提示词（整段复制给 AI）→ Open the development log prompt (copy the whole thing to AI)

20你的亲手部分：建结构 + push 到 GitHubYour Part By Hand: Build the Structure + Push to GitHub

用老师提供的 template repo 新建你自己的 private repo，然后：Create your own private repo from the instructor's template, then:

① 填内容（网页编辑即可）① Fill the content (edit on the web)

把 Brief 粘进 brief.md；Canva 链接写进 graph.md；导出的问题评分 CSV 上传为 ratings.csv。Paste your brief into brief.md; put the Canva link in graph.md; upload the exported ratings CSV as ratings.csv.

② 把 AI 生成的成果放进去② Put AI's output in place

游戏文件放 game/，网站文件放 website/，开发日志放 development-log/。Game files go in game/, site files in website/, the development log in development-log/.

③ 用 GitHub Desktop 推送（三个动作）③ Push with GitHub Desktop (three moves)

改动文件 → commit（本地存档，写一句"这次改了什么"）→ push（上传到 GitHub）。不用记命令行。Change files → commit (save locally, write what changed) → push (upload to GitHub). No command line needed.

④ 把老师加为 collaborator④ Add your instructor as collaborator

Settings → Collaborators，一次点击。之后老师就能看到你的进度了。Settings → Collaborators, one click. Your instructor can then see your progress.

为什么要亲手做这一步：建文件夹 = 理解一个游戏项目由什么组成；commit / push = 理解"存档 + 上传"的版本概念。这两件事是知识框架的一部分，不外包给 AI。Why do this by hand: building the folders teaches you what a game project is made of; commit/push teaches the versioning idea of "save + upload." Both are part of the knowledge framework — do not outsource them.

20b顺手给每道题打个分Rate Each Question While You Go

每个问题页底部有 5 颗星：这题问得清楚吗？你的评分只存在本地，不会进入交给 AI 的 Brief——它是用来帮老师改进问题设计的。写完所有问题后，点"导出问题评分 CSV"下载并上传。Every question page has 5 stars at the bottom: is this question clear? Your ratings stay local and do not go into the brief you hand to AI — they help the instructor improve the questions. When you finish, click "Export Question Ratings CSV" to download and upload it.

21设计者陈述模板Designer Statement Template

我的游戏来自我对 ____ 领域的学习。我希望玩家从"____"的新手理解，转向"____"的领域判断。我的核心机制是 ____。玩家通过 ____ 反馈，逐渐学会 ____。第一版挑战组合是 ____，它考验的是 ____。 My game comes from my learning of ____. I want the player to move from the novice understanding "____" to the domain judgment "____". My core mechanic is ____. Through ____ feedback, the player gradually learns ____. My first challenge set is ____, and it tests ____.

自检清单Self-check

  • 玩家能在 30 秒内做出一个有意义的行动The player can take a meaningful action within 30 seconds
  • 玩家的操作能明显改变系统数据或状态Player actions visibly change system data or state
  • 不可见的重要数据被翻译成可读的反馈Important invisible data is translated into readable feedback
  • 第二次玩会因为学到了反馈而做得更好A second attempt improves because the player learned from feedback
  • 挑战之间的差异来自变量、关系、信息或约束，而不是难度数字Challenge presets differ through variables, relationships, information, or constraints — not difficulty numbers
  • 游戏能在浏览器里直接跑，不需要复杂安装The game runs in a browser without a complex install
