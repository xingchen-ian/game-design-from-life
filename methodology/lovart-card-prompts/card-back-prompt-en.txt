Design the back side for the "Life-to-Game Prototype Deck".

Language rule:
Use ENGLISH ONLY. Do not use Chinese text.

Purpose:
This is the shared card back for all 54 cards. The back must not reveal suit, category, rank, or card type.

Visual direction:
Clean educational design, modern tabletop prototype aesthetic, print-friendly, high readability, restrained and professional. It should feel like a design tool, not a casino deck.

Composition:
- Standard poker card ratio, 63mm x 88mm.
- Warm off-white or very light neutral background.
- Center title: Life-to-Game Prototype Deck.
- Optional small subtitle: Emotion · Conflict · Resource · Action.
- Use a symmetrical repeating pattern made from four abstract icons:
  heart/pulse for Emotion,
  scale or obstacle mark for Conflict,
  diamond/token for Resource,
  clover/arrow for Action.
- Use all four accent colors subtly:
  heart red, dark blue-black, amber-orange, green.
- Add a thin border that matches the front card style.
- Keep enough empty space; avoid clutter.

Important constraints:
- The back must look identical for every card.
- Do not include rank, suit, category, or any hidden information.
- Avoid casino ornament, joker faces, fantasy art, childish cartoon style, and overly decorative patterns.
- Make it usable in black-and-white print: pattern should still be visible through shape, not only color.
