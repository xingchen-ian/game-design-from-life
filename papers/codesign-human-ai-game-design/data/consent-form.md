### English Version

#### 1. Purpose of the Study

This study aims to understand how students collaborate with AI assistants (e.g., Claude Code, WorkBuddy, Cursor) to design games in a game design course. We are particularly interested in how humans and AI divide labour and make design decisions, in order to improve game design education.

#### 2. What I Will Be Asked to Do

You only need to complete your normal coursework. As part of the course, you will produce the following materials, which the researcher wishes to analyse:

- Development log (`agent-development-log.md`) — recording your collaboration with the AI
- System design diagram
- Code repository (GitHub)
- Playable game prototype and screenshots
- Playtest notes

**No additional work is required** — all of the above are standard course deliverables.

#### 3. How the Data Will Be Used

- Academic journal publication (anonymous double-blind peer review) and conference presentations
- Improving future game design teaching

#### 4. Anonymisation

In publication:
- A project code (e.g., P01, P02) will replace your real name
- Identifying information (GitHub username, personal photos) will be removed
- Your game screenshots will only be shown with your approval

#### 5. Voluntary Participation and Right to Withdraw

- Participation is entirely voluntary
- You may withdraw your consent at **any time**, with **no impact on your course grade**
- After withdrawal, your data will no longer be used for future publication (already-published content excepted)

#### 6. Risks and Benefits

- **Risks**: Minimal. This study analyses routine coursework only, with no additional experiment or intervention.
- **Benefits**: No direct financial compensation. The research may improve game design education for future students.
